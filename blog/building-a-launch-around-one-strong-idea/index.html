Building a Launch Around One Strong Idea

A launch needs a sequence, a clear purpose and a story that holds together across every touchpoint.

Choose the job of the launch

Agree the audience, the message and the action you want to encourage. These choices shape the campaign. A business introduction, a new product and a repositioning may each need a different mix of media, partnerships and owned content.

Design the sequence

Work backwards from the launch date. Confirm the facts, prepare spokespeople and collect the required assets. Give each stage a purpose, from the first introduction through the announcement to the follow-up conversation.

Review what changed

Decide how you will assess the work before it goes live. Look at whether the right people encountered the story, whether the key message came through and what useful conversations followed. Use the review to inform the next campaign.
