Turning a Brand Story into a Media Conversation

A useful PR story starts with a reason for someone else to care. The announcement comes after the insight.

Start with the audience

Before drafting a release, ask whose attention you want and why the story matters to them. A founder, a customer and an editor may each see a different reason to care. A focused campaign gives each audience a clear entry point.

Find the specific story

Look for a real change, a useful observation or a distinctive perspective. Explain what is happening and what makes it relevant now. Replace sweeping statements with facts that can be checked, and prepare the evidence before making the pitch.

Make the next step easy

A concise brief, approved imagery and an available spokesperson help a journalist assess the story. Keep the pitch personal and relevant, respect editorial independence, and treat each exchange as the beginning of a relationship.
