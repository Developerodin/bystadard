Discuss this plan ↗
How does the programme work?

Start with a consultation brief. We shape the narrative and publication wishlist, agree a written proposal, then prepare content and coordinate delivery.

What does membership cover?

Strategy, story development, editing and publication coordination. Placements are purchased separately, from an indicative $495 each.

Which membership terms can I choose?

Month-to-month at $2,500 per month; three months at $1,950 per month; or twelve months at $990 per month. Fees are in USD. Publication costs and optional services are additional.

What is guaranteed?

Only deliverables explicitly accepted in a written agreement. Any placement refund terms apply to the contracted placement fee, not the membership. Publication availability must be confirmed before purchase.

How quickly can coverage appear?

Timing depends on editorial review, publication, edition and approval of your story. Your proposal includes an estimated schedule. Expedited delivery can be requested, subject to acceptance.

Are the listed publications partners?

The explorer is a wishlist of publication targets, not a claim of affiliation or confirmed access. Your proposal identifies the exact title, edition, format and availability.

Can I approve the article?

The proposed workflow includes your review of factual details and messaging before submission. Publishers retain their own editorial requirements.

Can you support an immigration publicity brief?

We can help organise a media story and coverage documentation for review with your immigration adviser. Publicity does not establish eligibility or guarantee a visa outcome.

Will this guarantee rankings or AI recommendations?

No. We offer research, content and visibility work. Search platforms and AI systems determine their own results.

Does the consultation form book a meeting?

It prepares a downloadable consultation brief including your preferred time. A meeting is confirmed separately; no slot is reserved by downloading a brief.
